# 🛡️ Google Ads Budget Protector (Master Skill)

This AI system specializes in Search and Performance Max (PMax) campaigns. It halts budget waste through negative keyword discovery, Quality Score recovery, and asset group fatigue management.

## Core Audit Logic

1. **Negative Keyword Discovery**:
   - Scrape search term reports for high-spend, zero-conversion anomalies.
   - Output exact match negative keyword lists.

2. **Quality Score Drain Prevention**:
   - Identify ad groups with Quality Scores below 5/10.
   - Recommend landing page relevance updates and exact keyword injection in H1/H2 tags.

3. **Performance Max Asset Fatigue**:
   - Flag PMax asset groups running unchanged for >60 days with declining ROAS.
   - Suggest immediate video and headline asset rotation to break algorithmic staleness.

## Usage
Paste your Google Ads performance metrics or search term report and state:
`"Execute Google Ads Budget Protection Audit on the following data:"`
