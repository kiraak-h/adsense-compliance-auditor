## 1. Data Collection & Analytics
We collect telemetry, usage metrics, and network identifier structures to optimize platform experiences. This site utilizes third-party tracking cookies to run behavioral analysis frameworks.

## 2. Google AdSense & Vendor Disclosures
- This website partners with Google AdSense to display programmatic marketing placements.
- Google utilizes tracking mechanisms, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve customized creatives across user sessions based on previous visits to this and alternative digital properties.
- Users may opt out of personalized behavioral advertising delivery structures at any moment by adjusting their global browser parameters or visiting the [Google Ads Settings Page](https://google.com).

## 3. User Data Rights
Depending on your regional jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA), you are granted explicit data privacy protections. This includes your right to view, modify, delete, or completely restrict the sale or sharing of your personal profiling datasets. To trigger these controls, utilize the interactive privacy widgets embedded within our footer configuration menu or submit a formal deletion request through our standard contact forms.
